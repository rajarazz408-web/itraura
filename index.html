Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes. Every drop is a pure botanical extract distilled using traditional methods. Zero alcohol.

Do you ship globally?

Yes, we ship to UAE, UK, and USA. Contact our concierge via WhatsApp for international rates.

How long does the scent last?

Being pure oils, they evolve on the skin and typically last 8-12 hours.

What is the Deg-Bhapka method?

It is a 400-year-old steam distillation art using copper vessels to capture the soul of flowers.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes, our botanical extracts are skin-friendly, but a patch test is always recommended.

Can I use these for meditation?

Absolutely. Our oils are 100% therapeutic and pure, making them ideal for spiritual and aromatic use.
