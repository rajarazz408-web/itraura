Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes. Every drop is a pure botanical extract distilled using traditional Deg-Bhapka methods. Zero alcohol.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we offer secure shipping to UAE, UK, and USA. Contact our concierge via WhatsApp for rates.

How long does the scent last?

Being pure oils, they evolve on the skin and typically last 8-12 hours.

What is the Deg-Bhapka method?

It is a 400-year-old steam distillation art using copper vessels to capture the soul of flowers.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes, botanical oils are skin-friendly, but a patch test is recommended for pure extracts.

Are your oils therapeutic?

Absolutely. Our oils are 100% pure botanical extracts, ideal for meditation and spiritual use.
