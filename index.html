Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes, absolutely. Every single drop we produce is a pure botanical extract distilled using traditional Deg-Bhapka methods. We use zero alcohol and zero synthetic chemicals in our entire collection.

Do you ship to UAE, UK & USA?

Yes, we offer secure global shipping for our international connoisseurs. Our team ensures that the delicate botanical oils are packed with extreme care. Please contact our concierge for specific international rates.

How long does the scent typically last?

Since these are 100% pure oils, they evolve on the skin over time. Depending on your skin chemistry and ambient temperature, the fragrance typically lasts between 8 to 12 hours with a beautiful sillage.

What makes the Deg-Bhapka method so special?

It is a 400-year-old ancient steam distillation process using copper vessels (Degs) and water-filled receivers (Bhapkas). This slow fire method captures the true therapeutic soul of the flower which modern machines cannot replicate.

Is it safe for extremely sensitive skin?

Our botanical extracts are natural and skin-friendly. However, because they are highly concentrated pure essences, we always recommend a small patch test on your wrist before full application.

Where do you source your flowers from?

We source our raw materials directly from our own and partner organic fields in Hasayan and Kannauj. This allows us to control the quality from the harvest to the final distillation since 1958.
