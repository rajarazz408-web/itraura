Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes. Every drop is a pure botanical extract distilled using traditional Deg-Bhapka methods. Zero alcohol, zero synthetics.

Do you ship to UAE, UK & USA?

Yes, we offer secure global shipping for international connoisseurs. Contact our concierge via WhatsApp for shipping rates.

How long does the scent typically last?

Pure oils evolve on the skin and typically last 8-12 hours depending on ambient temperature and skin type.

What is the Deg-Bhapka method?

It is a 400-year-old steam distillation process using copper vessels (Degs) to capture the true therapeutic soul of flowers.

Is it safe for extremely sensitive skin?

Our botanical extracts are natural and skin-friendly. However, a small patch test is always recommended for pure oils.

Where do you source your flowers from?

We source our materials directly from organic fields in Hasayan and Kannauj to ensure heritage quality since 1958.
