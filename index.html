Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes. Every drop is a pure botanical extract distilled using traditional Deg-Bhapka methods. Zero alcohol, zero synthetics.

Do you ship to UAE, UK & USA?

Yes, we offer secure global shipping for our international connoisseurs. Contact concierge via WhatsApp for shipping rates.

How long does the scent last?

Being pure oils, they evolve on the skin and typically last 8-12 hours depending on ambient temperature.

What is the Deg-Bhapka method?

It is a 400-year-old steam distillation process using copper vessels (Degs) to capture the true soul of flowers.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes, our botanical extracts are skin-friendly and therapeutic, but a patch test is always recommended.

Are your oils organic?

We source our raw materials directly from organic fields in Hasayan and Kannauj to ensure heritage quality.
