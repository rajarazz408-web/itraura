Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes. Every drop is a pure botanical extract distilled using traditional 400-year-old methods. We use zero synthetic alcohol or fixatives.

Do you ship to UAE, UK, and USA?

Yes, we offer secure global shipping to London, Dubai, New York, and beyond. Please contact our WhatsApp concierge for real-time international rates.

What makes Deg-Bhapka special?

It is a slow, artisanal steam distillation process using copper stills. It captures therapeutic properties and delicate aromatic molecules that modern machinery cannot preserve.

Are these perfumes safe for sensitive skin?

Our oils are 100% botanical and free from synthetic parabens and phthalates. However, we always recommend a patch test as pure botanical oils are highly concentrated.

How long does the scent last?

Being pure oils, they evolve on the skin and typically last much longer (8-12 hours) than alcohol-based sprays, depending on the base notes.
