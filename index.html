Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes, absolutely. We preserve the ancient Deg-Bhapka tradition. Every drop is a pure botanical extract with zero alcohol, zero synthetics, and zero parabens.

What makes Deg-Bhapka distillation special?

It is a 400-year-old traditional steam distillation process using copper vessels (Degs). Unlike modern machinery, this slow process captures the true 'soul' and therapeutic properties of the flowers.
