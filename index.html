Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes. Every drop is a pure botanical extract distilled using traditional Deg-Bhapka methods. Zero alcohol, zero synthetics.

Do you ship to UAE, UK & USA?

Yes, we offer secure global shipping for international connoisseurs. Contact concierge via WhatsApp for rates.

How long does the scent typically last?

Pure oils evolve on the skin and typically last 8-12 hours depending on ambient temperature and skin type.

What is the Deg-Bhapka method?

It is a 400-year-old steam distillation process using copper vessels (Degs) to capture the true soul of flowers.

Is it safe for extremely sensitive skin?

Our botanical extracts are skin-friendly, but because they are highly concentrated, a patch test is always recommended.

Where do you source your flowers from?

We source materials directly from organic fields in Hasayan and Kannauj to ensure heritage quality since 1958.
