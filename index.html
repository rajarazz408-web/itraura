Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes. We follow the strict 400-year-old Deg-Bhapka tradition. Every drop is a pure botanical extract with zero synthetics.

Do you ship to UAE, UK, and USA?

Yes, we offer secure global shipping to London, Dubai, and beyond. Contact our concierge on WhatsApp for international rates.

What is the Deg-Bhapka process?

It is a traditional steam distillation method using copper vessels (Degs) and clay-sealed receivers (Bhapkas) to capture the true soul of flowers.

Are these perfumes safe for sensitive skin?

Our oils are 100% botanical and free from harsh chemicals, making them much gentler than alcohol-based sprays.

How long does the scent last?

Being concentrated pure oils, our fragrances evolve on the skin and typically last much longer than commercial EDTs or EDPs.
