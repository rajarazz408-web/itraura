Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes. Every drop is a pure botanical extract distilled using traditional Deg-Bhapka methods. Zero alcohol.

Do you ship to UAE & UK?

Yes, we offer secure global shipping. Contact concierge for international rates.

How long does the scent last?

Being pure oils, they typically last 8-12 hours on skin.

What is the Deg-Bhapka method?

It is a 400-year-old steam distillation process that captures the soul of flowers.

Safe for sensitive skin?

Yes, botanical oils are skin-friendly, but a patch test is recommended.

Are your oils organic?

Yes, we source directly from organic rose fields in Hasayan to ensure 100% purity.
