Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes. Every drop is a pure botanical extract distilled using traditional Deg-Bhapka methods. Zero alcohol, zero synthetics, making it completely skin-friendly and therapeutic.

Do you ship to UAE, UK & USA?

Yes, we offer secure global shipping for international connoisseurs across Dubai, London, and the USA. Contact our concierge via WhatsApp for real-time international freight rates.

How long does the scent typically last?

Pure traditional attars and essential oils evolve on the skin based on body chemistry and typically last 8 to 12 hours depending on ambient temperature and skin type.

What is the Deg-Bhapka method?

It is a 400-year-old traditional steam distillation process using heavy copper vessels (Degs) and clay receivers (Bhapkas) to safely capture the true botanical soul of flowers without chemical solvents.

Is it safe for extremely sensitive skin?

Our botanical extracts and pure desi itr are 100% organic and chemical-free. However, because they are highly concentrated, a minor patch test on the wrist is always recommended.

Where do you source your flowers from?

We source our premium materials directly from organic agricultural fields in Hasayan and Kannauj to ensure heritage quality distillation since 1958.
