Heritage Since 1958.

At ItrAura, purity is our inheritance. Born from a family tradition established in 1958, we preserve the 400-year-old Deg-Bhapka steam distillation method in Hasayan.

Each bottle is a masterpiece of Hand-crafted, 100% Botanical essence, bringing the soul of Indian flowers to the global stage.

Essential Inquiries

Are ItrAura fragrances 100% alcohol-free?

Yes. Every drop is a pure botanical extract distilled using traditional Deg-Bhapka methods. No synthetic alcohol.

Do you ship to UAE & UK?

Yes, we offer secure global shipping for our international connoisseurs. Contact concierge for rates.

How long does the scent last?

Being pure oils, they evolve on the skin and typically last 8-12 hours depending on the notes.

Safe for sensitive skin?

Yes, botanical oils are skin-friendly, but a patch test is always recommended for pure extracts.
